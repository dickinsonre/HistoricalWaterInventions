### 4.2 Chain Pump (翻车/龙骨水车)

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  CHAIN PUMP (翻车 Fānchē / 龙骨水车 Lónggǔ Shuǐchē)                          ║
║  Ancient China • ~200 CE (Han Dynasty)                                       ║
╠══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║                                                                              ║
║  OVERVIEW:                                                                   ║
║  The Chinese chain pump, also called "dragon backbone water lift" due to    ║

### 5.1 Tenochtitlan Dike System (Albarradón de Nezahualcóyotl)
