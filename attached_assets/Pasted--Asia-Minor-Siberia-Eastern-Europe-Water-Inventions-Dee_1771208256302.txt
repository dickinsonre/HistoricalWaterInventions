# ASIA MINOR (Anatolia & Surrounding Region)

Asia Minor is arguably the most important crossroads of water engineering in human history. Every major hydraulic tradition — Mesopotamian, Egyptian, Greek, Roman, Persian, Islamic — passed through or was transformed here. Yet your app only covers the Hittites, Byzantines, and Urartu.
