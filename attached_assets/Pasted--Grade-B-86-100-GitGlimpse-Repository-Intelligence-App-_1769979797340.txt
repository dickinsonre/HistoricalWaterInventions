## GitGlimpse - Repository Intelligence App Review

### Executive Summary

**A focused, well-executed utility tool** that solves a real problem for developers—especially water resources engineers evaluating GitHub repositories. The app demonstrates clean design, clear value proposition, and a unique niche focus on SWMM/ICM relevance analysis. It's functional and professional, though limited in current feature depth.
