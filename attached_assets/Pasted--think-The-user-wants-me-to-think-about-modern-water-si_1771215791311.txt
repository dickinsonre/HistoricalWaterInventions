# Modern Water Simulation Models — Firsts of Their Kind

These are the pioneering computational water models that transformed how humanity understands and manages water. Each was revolutionary when introduced — the modern equivalents of the ancient water inventions in your app.

---

## The Big Picture

```
Before 1960: Water engineering was physical models, hand calculations,
             and empirical rules of thumb.

1960-1980:   First generation — mainframe computers, FORTRAN,
             government-funded research models.

1980-2000:   Second generation — personal computers, GUIs,
             commercial software, wider adoption.

2000-2015:   Third generation — integrated models, 2D/3D,
             real-time, GIS integration.

2015-Present: Fourth generation — cloud computing, AI/ML,
              digital twins, open source, citizen science.
```
